# User Manual — Gift of the Givers Disaster Alleviation Platform

*Written for non-technical staff at the Foundation. Replace the bracketed
`[screenshot: …]` placeholders with images of the running application.*

## 1. Logging in

There are three ways to use the platform:

- **Employee** — foundation staff. Go to **Login**, enter your work email and password.
  - Demo: `employee@giftofthegivers.org` / `Employee@123`
- **Donor** — a supporter with an account. Click **Register** to create one, or **Login** if you already have one.
  - Demo: `donor@giftofthegivers.org` / `Donor@123`
- **Anonymous guest** — no account needed. Just go to **Donate** and tick *"Donate anonymously"*.

`[screenshot: Login page]`

## 2. Posting a project update (Employee)

1. Log in as an employee — you land on the **Operations Dashboard**.
2. Click **Post Project Update** (top right).
3. Choose the relief project, enter a **Title** and the **update details**, then click **Publish Update**.
4. The update now appears under that project on the dashboard.

`[screenshot: Employee dashboard]` `[screenshot: Post update form]`

## 3. Making a donation

1. Click **Donate** in the top menu.
2. Choose **One-time** or **Recurring (monthly)**. For recurring, enter the number of months.
3. Enter the **Amount** and pick a **Currency** (ZAR, USD or EUR).
4. Either fill in your **name & email**, or tick **Donate anonymously** to give as a guest.
5. Click **Complete Donation**.

`[screenshot: Donation form]`

## 4. Generating / downloading a tax certificate

- After completing a donation you are taken to a **confirmation page** showing your
  certificate number and total.
- Click **Download Tax Certificate (PDF)** to save the placeholder certificate.

`[screenshot: Donation confirmation with certificate]`

## 5. Registering as a volunteer

1. Click **Volunteer** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **name, email, skills** and choose your **availability**.
3. Click **Register Interest** — you'll see a thank-you confirmation, and staff can view
   your details on the dashboard.

`[screenshot: Volunteer form]`

---

*If you get stuck, contact the Foundation IT team. Employees only can access the dashboard;
if you see an "Access Denied" page you are not logged in with an employee account.*
